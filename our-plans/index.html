Understanding Connections

How Many Connections Does Your Brampton Household Need?

A connection is the number of devices that can stream at the same time under one subscription. Here is a quick guide to choosing the right tier for your home.

1 Connection — Perfect for Individuals

If you live alone in Brampton or you are the only person in your household who watches IPTV, the single-connection plan gives you everything you need. You can install the app on as many devices as you like — your Firestick in the living room, your phone for commuting on the GO Train, your laptop at the office — but only one device streams at a time. Switch between them as often as you want at no extra cost.

2 Connections — Great for Couples

Two people, two screens, two different channels at the same time. One of you watches live sports in the basement while the other catches up on a series in the bedroom — both in full 4K. The 2-connection plan is our most popular tier among couples and small households across Brampton and Mississauga who want flexibility without overpaying.

3 Connections — Built for Families

Families with kids, roommates sharing a house, or anyone who needs three simultaneous streams will want the 3-connection plan. Three people watching three completely different things on three separate devices, all running smoothly at the same time. It is the plan most Brampton families choose, and the annual pricing keeps it remarkably affordable for what you get.
