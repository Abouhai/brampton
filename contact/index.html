Tips for Faster Support

How to Get the Fastest Help From Our Team

We want every interaction to be quick and painless. To help us help you as fast as possible, here are a few simple things you can include in your first message on WhatsApp or in your email.

  • Your subscription username or email address so we can pull up your account instantly without having to ask
  • The device you are using — Firestick, Android box, Samsung Smart TV, iPhone, Windows PC, or whatever you are streaming on
  • The IPTV player app you have installed, such as IPTV Smarters, TiviMate or Edge IPTV
  • A brief description of the issue — what you see on screen, when it started, and whether it affects all channels or just specific ones
  • A screenshot or short video if the problem is visual — this helps our team diagnose the issue without a back-and-forth guessing game

Including these details upfront means we can often resolve your issue in a single reply rather than needing to ask follow-up questions first.
